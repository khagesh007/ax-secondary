Agent will initiate the deals creation from either deals or property tab.

Agent has to fill following details.

	Property Listing.
	Owner information will be extracted from property relation.
	Lead selection. Can be multple leads
		Lead can be of multiple type.(Individual or Company).
			In Case of Company Lead
				Trade License
				Company POA/Signatory (POA Letter, POA Passport Copy, Visa & Emirates ID)
				Memorandum of Association
				Certificate of Incumbency
				VAT Certificate
				Authorize Signatory Document – Owner’s docs as per TL (Passport, Visa & EID)
				Plus all of the above documents



		Upon selection of a lead all the necessary information has to be filled for all the leads selected.
			Basic Details
			Full Name.
			Passport Number.
			Nationality.
			Address.
			Country of residence(Have to add this field).
			Lead Source.
			Occupation Details
			Source of Funds
				eg
				1. Empoloyement Income
				2. Sale of investment or property.
				3. Business Income.
				4. Savings
				5. Other (add input field).

			Resident (Yes, No)
			Emirated Id Number for Uae nationals and UAE residents.

			Deal Type (Leasing Deal, Secondary, Off Plan)
				Documents(Leasing Deal)
					TD/OQOOD 
					Seller Passport (Agent to Add)
					Signed Contract A (Admin to add)
					Signed Contract B (Admin to add)
				Documents(Secondary)
					TD/OQOOD 
					Seller Passport (Agent to Add)
					Signed Contract A (Admin to add)
					Signed Contract B (Admin to add)
				Documents(Off Plan)
					Booking Form
					Buyer Passport, Visa & EID 
					A to A Agreement (If applicable)


After all the information is filled by Agent Admin will get the notification and admin will have to fill out details required from there side.
